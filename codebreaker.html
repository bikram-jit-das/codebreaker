What English looks like

From about 3 million letters of War and Peace: how often each letter is followed by each other letter. This table is what the computer uses to score a guess.

Letter-pair fingerprint

Hover or tap a square.

Rows: current letter. Columns: next letter. "_" means space. Darker means more common.

Which looks most like English?

TextScoreLog score

Score = the chances of every neighbouring pair multiplied together (including the spaces at the start and end). Higher means more English-like. The log score is easier for computers: closer to 0 is better.
